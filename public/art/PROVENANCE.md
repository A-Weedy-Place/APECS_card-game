## Active assets — 28 October 2026

All 51 cards now have different local real photographs in `cards/`, approximately 4.3 MB combined. The keyed `cards/credits.json` records each original source, full caption, author, licence/link, file hash, dimensions and resizing/compression changes. Each photograph has a `.licence.txt` sidecar and is credited in card details and `/credits`. The background remains the NASA southwest Greenland photograph from the 27 October import below. No proprietary game art or newly generated image is used for the current card art.

Subjects include Paris Agreement adoption (2015), COP21/26/27, Rio+20, the UN Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues (2015), the Copenhagen COP15 march (2009), the Dili climate strike (2019), and White/Keynes at the IMF (1946, not the 1944 Bretton Woods conference), alongside appropriate facility, nature and technology photos. Historic people/events illustrate fictional game scenarios and do not imply endorsement or that those people adopted the depicted card. Fifty sources are Wikimedia Commons shared files; one is the existing NASA training photograph. Reuse is limited to the recorded CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public-domain terms. Adapted photographs retain applicable share-alike terms; their licences do not relicense unrelated game code.

`scripts/acquire-card-photos.mjs` reproduces the curated asset import and keeps cached files unless `--replace` is explicitly passed. Commons website DNS failed in this environment. The official English Wikipedia Action API supplied shared-file `imageinfo` and the Wikimedia upload/thumbnail CDNs supplied image bytes. Requests identify the project, are paced, and honour rate-limit/backoff guidance: https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_APIs/Rate_limits and https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_APIs/API_access_policy . No system network settings were changed. No manual downloads are outstanding. `scripts/photo-contact-sheet.mjs` generates a local review sheet.

Manual licence review corrected the European Parliament photo to its required **© European Union 2023 – Source: EP, CC BY 4.0** credit, and a methane pipeline photograph to **© Andrew Dunn 2005, CC BY-SA 2.0** after its machine-readable author field was blank. An uncertain private Rio 1992 news image, a night-time Eiffel Tower image, a direct-air-capture diagram and an agroecology collage were rejected/replaced. The final direct-air-capture card uses a real Climeworks facility photo; agroforestry uses a real Faidherbia albida tree photo. The solar card uses the Jännersdorf photovoltaic field (CC BY-SA 3.0) in preference to a valid but less recognisable molten-salt storage photo. Automated tests check 51 distinct local file hashes and non-empty author/licence/source records; the browser checks distinct asset URLs and decoded visible library photos. Dimensions are retained in source metadata.
